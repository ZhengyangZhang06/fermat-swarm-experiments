## Proof

1. Fix M, u, ζ, and the stated hypothesis. The group Gal(M/ℚ) is finite. Let H be the cyclic subgroup generated by u and let F = M^H. If m is the positive order of u, every element of H is u^n for some natural n with 0 ≤ n < m, since any integer exponent can be reduced modulo m.
2. The fixed field F is an intermediate field of M/ℚ. Finite Galois correspondence gives Gal(M/F) = H under the identification that forgets F-linearity. In particular M/F is finite Galois. Because u belongs to H, it fixes F pointwise; its existing field automorphism therefore defines an F-algebra automorphism h of M. By construction h(x) = u(x) for every x ∈ M.
3. Let K = F(ζ), the intermediate field generated over F by ζ. Every K-automorphism σ of M fixes F, so its underlying rational automorphism belongs to H. By step 1, it equals u^n for some natural n. It also fixes ζ because ζ belongs to K. The hypothesis therefore gives u^n = 1, and hence σ is the identity. Thus Gal(M/K) is trivial.
4. Since M/F is finite Galois and K is intermediate, M/K is finite Galois. Its degree equals its automorphism-group cardinality, which is one by step 3. Hence K = M, or IntermediateField.adjoin F {ζ} = ⊤.
5. The field F and automorphism h constructed in step 2 satisfy this adjoin equality by step 4 and satisfy the required pointwise equality by construction. They are the desired witnesses.

## Key steps

1. Form the finite cyclic subgroup generated by u and its fixed field F.
2. Regard u as an F-automorphism h with the same underlying function.
3. Show every automorphism fixing F and ζ is a nonnegative power of u and hence the identity.
4. Use finite Galois correspondence to conclude F(ζ) = M.
