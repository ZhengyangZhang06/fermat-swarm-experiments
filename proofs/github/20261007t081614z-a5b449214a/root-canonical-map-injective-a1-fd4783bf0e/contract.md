## Theorem `Submission.p05_canonical_map_injective_a5b449214a`

Let k be a field, H a commutative Hopf k-algebra, and K a k-subalgebra such that Δ_H(K) lies in the k-linear span of tensors with both factors in K and S_H(K)⊆K. Let B be a commutative k-bialgebra and q:H→B a surjective bialgebra homomorphism whose kernel is generated by {t∈K:ε_H(t)=0}. Give H its K-algebra structure by inclusion and put ρ=(id_H⊗q)Δ_H. There exists an injective k-algebra homomorphism β:H⊗_K H→H⊗_k B such that β(a⊗_K b)=(a⊗_k1_B)ρ(b) for all a,b∈H.

## Proof

1. Put T=H⊗_K H and ρ=(id_H⊗q)Δ_H. Apply the subalgebra_coinvariant sibling with hΔ and hker. It gives ρ(t)=t⊗1_B for every t∈K. Since Δ_H and q are algebra homomorphisms, ρ is a k-algebra homomorphism.
2. The tensor algebra T has multiplication (a⊗b)(a'⊗b')=aa'⊗bb' and unit 1⊗1. These formulas respect K-balancing: replacing at⊗b by a⊗tb changes the product to aa't⊗bb' or aa'⊗tbb', which are balanced equal; commutativity gives the check in the other input. Ring identities follow on pure tensors and extend by distributivity. Its k-scalar image of λ is (λ1_H)⊗1=1⊗(λ1_H).
3. The formula (a,b)↦(a⊗1_B)ρ(b) is biadditive and k-bilinear. For t∈K, step 1 gives ρ(tb)=(t⊗1_B)ρ(b), so its values at (at,b) and (a,tb) agree. It is also K-linear in the first variable when the target has the K-action through its first H-factor. The balanced tensor universal property therefore gives a k-linear β:T→H⊗_k B with the required formula. Multiplicativity of ρ and commutativity prove preservation of products on pure tensors and hence on sums. Its value at 1⊗1 is 1, so β is a k-algebra homomorphism.
4. The antipode S of H is a k-algebra homomorphism. Define convolution of linear maps H⊗H→H by (f*g)(a⊗b)=Σ f(a_1⊗b_1)g(a_2⊗b_2). Coassociativity makes it associative, with identity e(a⊗b)=ε(a)ε(b)1. For f(a⊗b)=ab, g(a⊗b)=S(ab), and h(a⊗b)=S(a)S(b), multiplicativity of Δ and ε and the antipode identity for ab give g*f=e. Commutativity and the two antipode identities give f*h=e. Thus g=g*(f*h)=(g*f)*h=h, proving multiplicativity. The identity at 1 gives S(1)=1, and S is already k-linear.
5. Define τ:H→T by τ(b)=Σ S(b_1)⊗_K b_2. This is Δ_H followed by S⊗id on the tensor product over k and then the natural map H⊗_k H→H⊗_K H. The latter exists because K-balancing includes k-balancing, and its pure-tensor formula preserves products and the unit. Step 4 therefore makes τ a k-algebra homomorphism.
6. For t∈K, hΔ gives Δ_H(t)=Σ_i a_i⊗b_i with a_i,b_i∈K after absorbing scalar coefficients. By hS, S(a_i)∈K. Balancing and the antipode identity give τ(t)=Σ_i1⊗_K S(a_i)b_i=1⊗_K algebraMap(k,H)(ε_H(t)), the scalar image of ε_H(t) in T. Thus τ kills every t∈K of zero counit. Its kernel is an ideal containing these generators, so hker implies ker(q)⊆ker(τ).
7. For c∈B choose b∈H with q(b)=c, using hq, and set τ̄(c)=τ(b). Two lifts differ by an element of ker(q), which τ kills, so the value is independent of the choice. Sums and products of lifts prove additivity and multiplicativity. Lifts 1_H and the scalar images prove preservation of the unit and k-scalars. Consequently τ̄:B→T is a k-algebra homomorphism with τ̄(q(b))=τ(b).
8. Define a k-linear map γ:H⊗_k B→T by γ(a⊗c)=(a⊗_K1)τ̄(c); the formula is k-bilinear. Expanding β and τ̄ and using coassociativity gives γβ(a⊗_K b)=Σ ab_1S(b_2)⊗_K b_3. In H⊗_k H, apply multiplication after id⊗S to the first two factors of (Δ_H⊗id)Δ_H(b). The antipode identity followed by the left counit identity gives Σ b_1S(b_2)⊗b_3=1⊗b. Coassociativity identifies this expression with the one in γβ. Map the identity to T and multiply its first factor by a to obtain γβ(a⊗_K b)=a⊗_K b.
9. Pure tensors generate T additively, so γβ=id_T. Applying γ to β(z)=β(z') gives z=z', proving injectivity. The formula in step 3 is precisely the required formula involving HopfAlgebra.coaction q. Thus β satisfies both conjuncts of the conclusion.

## Key steps

1. Obtain K-balancing from subalgebra_coinvariant.
2. Construct the canonical k-algebra homomorphism β.
3. Prove antipode multiplicativity and construct the translation map τ.
4. Show τ kills the augmentation generators and hence ker(q).
5. Descend τ through q and construct γ.
6. Use coassociativity and antipode cancellation to prove γβ=id.
7. Conclude injectivity and the prescribed pure-tensor formula.
