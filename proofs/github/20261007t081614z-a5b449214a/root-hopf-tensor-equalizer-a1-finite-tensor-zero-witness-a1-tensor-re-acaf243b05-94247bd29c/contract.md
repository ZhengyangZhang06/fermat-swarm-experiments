## Theorem `Submission.p05_ftzw_tensor_relation_kernel_a5b449214a`

Let D be a commutative ring and let M and P be D-modules with additive commutative group structures. Write G = FreeAbelianGroup(M × P) and [u,z] for its pair generator. Let T consist of all [0,z], [u,0], [u+v,z]−[u,z]−[v,z], [u,z+w]−[u,z]−[u,w], and [d•u,z]−[u,d•z], with u,v in M, z,w in P, and d in D. Let E:G→M⊗_D P be the additive homomorphism sending [u,z] to u⊗_D z. Then ker E is the additive subgroup generated by T.

## Proof

1. Fix D, M and P as in the statement, and use its notation G, T and [u,z]. Let R be the additive subgroup generated by T. The universal property of the free abelian group gives the additive homomorphism E:G→M⊗_D P sending [u,z] to u⊗_D z.
2. Every generator in T is killed by E. For the two zero forms this follows from 0⊗z=0 and u⊗0=0; for the two additivity forms it follows from additivity of the tensor in the indicated argument; for the balancing form it follows from (d•u)⊗z=u⊗(d•z). Since ker E is a subgroup containing T, R is contained in ker E.
3. Form the quotient additive group Q=G/R with projection π. Set β(u,z)=π([u,z]). The first two types of generators give β(0,z)=0 and β(u,0)=0. The next two give β(u+v,z)=β(u,z)+β(v,z) and β(u,z+w)=β(u,z)+β(u,w). The last gives β(d•u,z)=β(u,d•z). Thus β is biadditive and D-balanced.
4. These identities induce an additive homomorphism L:M⊗_D P→Q with L(u⊗z)=β(u,z). Explicitly, extend β to the free additive monoid on pairs by summation. Its values agree across the zero, additivity and balancing constructors of the pinned TensorProduct.Eqv by step 3; they agree across its commutativity constructor because Q is abelian. The map therefore factors through the defining additive-congruence quotient. This is precisely TensorProduct.liftAddHom in the pinned library.
5. On each pair generator, (L∘E)([u,z])=π([u,z]). Additive homomorphisms out of a free abelian group are determined by generators, so L∘E=π on all of G: the equality extends to sums and additive inverses.
6. If g belongs to ker E, then π(g)=L(E(g))=L(0)=0. The zero class of G/R is exactly R, so g belongs to R. Hence ker E is contained in R. Together with step 2, this proves the asserted equality ker E=AddSubgroup.closure T.

## Key steps

1. Evaluate pair generators as pure tensors and show that every defining relation is killed.
2. In the quotient by the generated subgroup, pair classes form a biadditive balanced map.
3. Descend this map through the pinned tensor presentation to an additive map into the quotient.
4. Identify its composite with free-group evaluation as the quotient projection.
5. Use the kernel of the quotient projection to prove the reverse kernel inclusion.
