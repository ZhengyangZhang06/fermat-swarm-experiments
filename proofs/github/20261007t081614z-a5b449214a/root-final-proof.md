# Final proof of `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer_eq_of_surjective_of_ker_eq_span`

## Statement and assumptions

Let `k : Type u` be a field. Let `H : Type v` be a commutative ring with a Hopf `k`-algebra structure and an instance of `Algebra.FiniteType k H`. Let `K : Subalgebra k H`. Write Δ, ε, and S for the coproduct, counit, and antipode of H. Assume:

1. For every x ∈ K, Δ(x) lies in the k-linear span of the tensors a ⊗ₖ b with a, b ∈ K (`hΔ`).
2. For every x ∈ K, S(x) ∈ K (`hS`).
3. `B : Type w` is a commutative ring and a `k`-bialgebra, and `q : H →ₐc[k] B` is a bialgebra homomorphism.
4. q is surjective (`hq`).
5. The ring-homomorphism kernel of q is the ideal generated by {x ∈ H | x ∈ K and ε(x) = 0} (`hker`).

The conclusion is equality of subalgebras `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer q = K`. All these hypotheses, universes, and the conclusion are retained in the Lean declaration. No finite generation of K, antipode on B, cocommutativity, or flatness hypothesis is introduced. The finite-type hypothesis on H is retained even though the approved lemmas used below prove the relevant implications without it.

## Complete root argument

1. The pinned definition of the Hopf kernel is the equalizer of the two k-algebra homomorphisms ρ and x ↦ x ⊗ₖ 1_B. Thus, by `HopfAlgebra.mem_hopfKer_iff`, x ∈ `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer q` means exactly ρ(x) = x ⊗ₖ 1_B. In particular, ρ is a unital algebra homomorphism, so ρ(1_H) = 1 in H ⊗ₖ B.
2. Apply approved lemma 2 with K, `hΔ`, `hS`, q, `hq`, and `hker`. This supplies β, its injectivity `hβ`, and its pure-tensor formula `hβtmul`. No additional hypotheses are needed.
3. Let x ∈ `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer q`. Evaluate β on the two tensors required by approved lemma 3:

   β(x ⊗_K 1_H) = (x ⊗ₖ 1_B)ρ(1_H) = (x ⊗ₖ 1_B)1 = x ⊗ₖ 1_B,

   β(1_H ⊗_K x) = (1_H ⊗ₖ 1_B)ρ(x) = 1ρ(x) = ρ(x) = x ⊗ₖ 1_B.

   The equality 1_H ⊗ₖ 1_B = 1 is `Algebra.TensorProduct.one_def` used backwards. Preservation of one is `map_one`; the two unit simplifications are `mul_one` and `one_mul`. The remaining equality is the symmetric form of the membership equation from step 1. This is precisely the sequence of rewrites in the Lean proof.
4. Injectivity of β gives x ⊗_K 1_H = 1_H ⊗_K x. Apply approved lemma 3 with K, `hΔ`, `hS`, and x to obtain x ∈ K. Since x was arbitrary, `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer q ≤ K`.
5. Apply approved lemma 1 with K, `hΔ`, q, and `hker` to get the reverse inclusion K ≤ `HopfAlgebra.hopfKer q`. The order on subalgebras is inclusion; `le_antisymm` therefore gives the asserted equality.

This closes the root argument using established dependency contracts. There is no unresolved geometric or algebraic obligation, and the target is not used in establishing any premise.
